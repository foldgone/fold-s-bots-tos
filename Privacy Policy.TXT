fold's bots — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, processed, stored, and used when you interact with any of foldgone's bots (collectively referred to as the "Bots").

By adding or using any of the Bots, you acknowledge that information may be processed as described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on which Bot and features you use, the Bots may process information provided through Discord.

This may include:

Discord User IDs.
Discord Server IDs.
Discord Channel IDs.
Discord Role IDs.
Voice channel information.
Commands and interactions sent to the Bots.
Bot configuration and server settings.
User preferences associated with Bot features.
Information necessary to maintain queues, playlists, statistics, or other requested features.
Error logs and technical information necessary to diagnose problems.

The Bots are not intended to collect information that is unnecessary for their functionality.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the Bots may be used to:

Provide and operate Bot features.
Respond to commands and interactions.
Store server-specific configurations.
Maintain music queues, playlists, or related functionality where applicable.
Track statistics or activity where a feature requires it.
Maintain voice-related functionality where applicable.
Diagnose bugs, errors, and technical problems.
Prevent abuse, exploitation, or unauthorized access.
Improve the reliability and functionality of the Bots.
Provide support when necessary.

Information will not intentionally be used for purposes unrelated to operating, maintaining, securing, or improving the Bots unless otherwise stated.

3. Discord Information

The Bots operate through Discord and therefore interact with information made available through Discord's API.

The Bots do not control Discord's own collection or processing of your information.

Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own policies and terms.

For more information, please review Discord's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

4. Messages and Commands

The Bots may receive messages, commands, or interaction data that are necessary for their operation.

Depending on the Bot and its configuration, message content may be processed temporarily to determine how a command should be handled.

The Bots do not intentionally store the contents of private conversations for unrelated purposes.

If a feature requires storing information from a command or interaction, that information may be retained for the operation of that feature.

5. Music Features

For Zero Music and any music-related functionality provided by the Bots, information such as song searches, requested tracks, queues, playlists, user IDs, and server IDs may be processed when necessary to provide the requested functionality.

Music-related services may also rely on third-party platforms or services to locate, retrieve, or stream content.

The Bots do not claim ownership of third-party music or media content.

6. Voice Features

For features involving Discord voice channels, the Bots may process information such as:

Voice channel IDs.
Server IDs.
User IDs.
Voice state information.
Join and leave events.
Other information required to provide voice-related features.

The Bots do not intentionally record or store voice conversations unless a specific feature explicitly states otherwise.

7. Data Storage

Some information may be stored in databases or configuration files in order for Bot features to function correctly.

Examples may include:

User statistics.
Server settings.
Bot configuration.
Playlists or saved preferences.
Usage-related information required by specific features.

The amount and type of stored information may change as the Bots are updated.

8. Data Retention

Information is generally retained only for as long as it is reasonably necessary to provide the relevant Bot functionality, maintain records, prevent abuse, or resolve technical issues.

Some information may remain stored until:

It is no longer required.
The relevant feature is removed.
The Bot is removed from a server and associated data is deleted according to the Bot's data-management practices.
A deletion request is received and can reasonably be fulfilled.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the feature involved.

We do not intentionally sell personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services when those services are required to operate the Bots.

Examples may include:

Database providers.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

Comply with applicable law.
Respond to lawful requests.
Protect the security of the Bots or their infrastructure.

Protect the rights and safety of users or the service.
